Build a palette around one color

Choose a favorite color, then add a lighter tint, a darker shade, and one accent. Try the accent in just a few small areas to draw attention to the part of the page you like best.

Make a coloring session your own

Start with the largest shapes, then zoom in for the details. In a friend room, take turns choosing colors or agree on a palette before you begin. Save a copy when you find a combination you love.
